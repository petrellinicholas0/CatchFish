Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

This is a template Privacy Policy provided as a starting point and has not been reviewed by a lawyer. It should be reviewed by qualified legal counsel before relying on it.

1. Information We Collect

When you upgrade to a paid plan, we collect and store the following account information via Supabase:

  • Your email address;
  • Your plan status (e.g., free, active subscriber);
  • A Stripe customer identifier associated with your subscription or purchase.

We do not collect or store your card number or other payment card details — those are sent directly to and handled by Stripe. Profile content you submit for analysis (photos, bios, and related text) is processed to generate your results but is not linked to your billing identity beyond what's needed to enforce your plan's usage limits.

2. Photo and Analysis Data

CatchFish does not store uploaded photos on our servers. When you upload a photo for analysis, it is:

  • Held temporarily in your browser's memory only, for the duration of your analysis request;
  • Sent directly to Anthropic's Claude API to generate your analysis results;
  • Not saved to any CatchFish database, server, or storage system.

Third-party processing: Photos and text you submit for analysis are processed by Anthropic (the provider of the AI model powering CatchFish). Per Anthropic's API data retention policy, submitted content is automatically deleted from Anthropic's systems within 30 days and is not used to train their AI models. In rare cases involving suspected policy violations, Anthropic may retain data for a longer period as described in their policies.

Local device storage: If you have analysis history enabled, a text-only summary of past results may be saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. This is not transmitted to or stored on our servers, and can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser data.

What we do store: We retain your email address and subscription status to manage your account and process payments through Stripe. We do not store your uploaded photos, bios, or analysis content in our database.

If you use the in-app flagging feature to report a result, we store your report reason, any optional note you provide, and your anonymous device ID to review and improve our AI moderation.

3. Anonymous Usage Analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to understand overall site traffic (such as page visits) and to see, in aggregate, how often each tool (Profile Analyzer, Email Check, Paper Check) is used. This tracking is anonymous and aggregate only — it never includes the content you submit (no photos, bio text, email text, or paper text), and is not tied to your individual identity or account.

4. Google Ads Conversion Tracking

We use Google Ads (gtag.js) conversion tracking to measure the performance of our ad campaigns — for example, whether a visit that came from one of our ads led to a signup or purchase. This is separate from the Vercel Web Analytics described above and does not change the account data practices described in Section 1 (email, plan status, Stripe customer ID) or the photo and analysis data practices described in Section 2: it never has access to the photos, bio text, email text, or paper text you submit for analysis. Google's own privacy policy governs how it handles the data this tag collects.

5. How We Use Your Information

We use the information above to provide and maintain the Service, including verifying your subscription status, granting access to paid features, and communicating with you about your account.

6. Payment Processing

All payments are processed directly by Stripe. Stripe collects and handles your card details and other payment information under its own privacy policy and security practices; we never see or store your full card number.

7. Data Storage

Account data (email, plan status, and Stripe customer ID) is stored in Supabase, our database provider. Access to this data is restricted to what's needed to operate the Service.

8. No Selling of Data

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We share data only with the service providers necessary to run CatchFish (such as Stripe for payments, Supabase for account storage, and Google Ads for ad conversion tracking), and only to the extent needed for them to perform their function.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active or as needed to comply with legal and billing obligations. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us — see below.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the Service after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

11. Contact Us

Questions about this policy or want to request account deletion? Contact us at support@catchfishapp.com.
